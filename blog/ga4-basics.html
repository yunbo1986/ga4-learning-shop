GA4 Events: A Practical Beginner's Guide

GA4 becomes much easier when you stop thinking in pages and start thinking in meaningful actions.

Everything starts with an event

In GA4, an event records something that happened: a page loaded, a product was viewed, an item entered the cart, or a form was submitted. The event name describes the action, while parameters add useful detail.

Example: add_to_cart is the action. Parameters such as currency, value, item name, and quantity explain what was added.

Choose clear names

Use Google's recommended event names when they fit your action. They make reports easier to understand and unlock features designed for ecommerce, leads, and engagement. Use lowercase words joined by underscores.

Parameters answer the next question

An event tells you what happened. Parameters tell you which product, how much it was worth, where the action occurred, or which article was selected. Plan only the details you will genuinely use.

Start with a measurement plan

Write down the business question, the user action that answers it, the event name, and the required parameters. Your demo shop already follows this pattern for product views, cart actions, checkout, purchase, and lead generation.
